Overview

Load a league, pick two teams and run the simulation. The model estimates each team's expected goals from attack/defense strength (goals + optional FBref xG blend, shrunk toward league priors), then simulates the match 100,000 times with Poisson-distributed scoring to produce probabilities for 1X2, over/under, both-teams-to-score and correct scores — plus fair odds you can compare against a bookmaker.

⚠️ For information only. These are statistical estimates, not predictions or betting advice. Football is highly random — even a strong model is often wrong, and bookmaker margins make consistent profit very difficult. Never bet more than you can afford to lose.

Under the EU ePrivacy rules this storage is "strictly necessary", which is why we show a notice rather than a consent pop-up. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may set technically necessary cookies of its own. You can clear or block storage in your browser settings; the site will still load but sign-in, history and caching will not work.
